05 · Wheels

Two SUVs, good tires

In Fairbanks, tires matter more than AWD. We pick up and return at Fairbanks airport, so there's no one-way fee.

  • Winter or studded tiresNon-negotiable. Confirm with the branch.
  • Engine block heater cordsPlug in overnight at −30°F.
  • 3 am pickup on Dec 25Call ahead so someone's at the counter.
  • Return by 6 am Dec 29Before the 7:03 am flight.
  • 2–3 snow driversPeople comfortable on ice.
  • Skip Murphy DomeToo rough for regular SUVs.
